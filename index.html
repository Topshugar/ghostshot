Local Network Web Service Mapper
By Topshugar | For Authorized Testing Only

What GhostShot Does

Discovers active web services on YOUR local network, captures screenshots, and builds a report.

Usage in Termux / Kali:

python3 ghostshot.py 192.168.1.1-20

Output: ./shots/ + ./report.html

Ports scanned: 80, 443, 8080, 8000
