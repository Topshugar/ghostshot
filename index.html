Wireless Intrusion Detection and Assessment Tool

1. Airgeddon Attack Overview

Airgeddon is a wireless attack framework used to create a fraudulent access point (Evil Twin), force client disconnection (Deauthentication), and capture credentials via a captive portal. This tool assists in identifying indicators of such attacks.

Indicators of Compromise:
• Repeated disconnections at short intervals
• Duplicate SSID broadcasts observed
• Unsolicited captive portal requesting WiFi credentials
• Abnormal signal strength with degraded throughput
